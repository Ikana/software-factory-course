# Cadence

Cadence is a course platform. Learners buy courses, and they can ask for their money back within limits.

## Language

### Buying and access

**Purchase**:
A single payment for a course, made by one learner for themselves or by a team buyer for a Team Licence.
_Avoid_: Order, transaction

**Enrollment**:
A learner's access to a course. It comes from an individual Purchase or from a claimed Team Seat.
_Avoid_: Access grant, subscription

**Team Licence**:
A Purchase of several Team Seats for one course, made by a team buyer.
_Avoid_: Bulk purchase

**Team Seat**:
One place in a Team Licence. A learner claims it with its code, and claiming it gives them an Enrollment.
_Avoid_: Coupon (the code is just how a seat is claimed)

**Gross Revenue**:
The total of every Purchase for an instructor's courses.
_Avoid_: Sales, income

**Net Revenue**:
Gross Revenue minus every Refund, counted from the moment its Refund Request is Approved. This is the main revenue figure an instructor sees.
_Avoid_: Earnings, profit

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A learner's request for their money back on their own individual Purchase. It is Requested, then either Approved or Declined. A Purchase can only ever have one.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback, cancel my purchase

**Refund Review**:
An admin's decision to approve or decline a Refund Request. Only a learner who has had a Refund before gets one. Every other eligible Refund Request is approved automatically.
_Avoid_: Manual approval, moderation

**Refund**:
Cadence's record that it owes a learner the full amount they paid, created when a Refund Request is Approved. An admin returns the money by hand. Approving a Refund Request ends the learner's Enrollment.
_Avoid_: Reversal, partial refund

**Paid Out**:
A Refund whose money an admin has actually sent back to the learner. Before that, the Refund is owed.
_Avoid_: Completed, processed, refunded

**Refund Window**:
The 14 calendar days after a Purchase during which a Refund Request may be made. The fourteenth day counts in full.
_Avoid_: Cancel window

**Refund Eligibility**:
A Purchase can have a Refund Request only if it is an individual Purchase (not a Team Licence), it is inside the Refund Window, the learner has watched under 2 hours and completed under half the lessons, it has never had a Refund Request before, and it is not a repeat Purchase of a course the learner was already refunded for.
_Avoid_: Cancellation offer

**Chargeback**:
A payment dispute that the learner's bank starts, not the learner on Cadence. It is not a Refund Request.
